How To

From a PowerPoint file to a finished video in a few steps.

Open your presentationOn Create, choose or drop a .pptx file. Slides, animations and transitions are read in your browser.
Add narrationOpen Narration under the slide: use the speaker notes, the text on the slide, your own recording, or let an AI write it. Pick a voice and press Generate all.
Choose background videosOpen Background video on the right. "Suggested" shows clips whose tags match the words on the slide.
Pick the transitionsClick the button between two thumbnails to preview and choose a transition – mattes are chosen at random by default.
ExportIn "Create video" choose the resolution and press Export video. Or press "Create a quick video" and let the wizard do everything.

Help

Answers to common questions.

Is my presentation uploaded anywhere?
No. The file is opened, rendered and exported inside your browser. Only the voice models and the background videos are downloaded to your computer.
Speech generation is slow
Voices run on your own computer. Piper is the fastest; Kokoro sounds more natural but needs more power. The first time, each voice is downloaded once and then kept by the browser.
Which browsers work?
Recent Chrome, Edge and Opera work best. Firefox and Safari can make the video in real time when fast export is not available.
Some fonts look different from PowerPoint
The browser uses the fonts installed on your computer. Install the presentation's fonts, or choose fonts that are common on Windows.
Where are my videos?
Under History, until you close this tab. Use Download to save them.
