The Technology

This isn't a simple resize filter

AI Video Upscaler+ is built on three real, named AI models — each handling a different part of turning old footage into something sharp and smooth.

Resolution AI

Real-ESRGAN

An AI super-resolution model that reconstructs real detail — edges, textures, skin, text — rather than just stretching or smoothing existing pixels.

Frame Rate AI

RIFE

An AI frame-interpolation model that generates genuinely new in-between frames from motion, turning choppy 24–30fps footage into smooth 45–60fps video.

Face Restoration AI

GFPGAN

An optional AI enhancement pass that sharpens and restores facial detail that low resolution or age can soften.

FAQ

Common questions

Is AI Video Upscaler+ really free?
Yes. Every feature is available with no subscription, no per-video charges, and no watermark on your output.
Does AI Video Upscaler+ send my videos anywhere?
No. Source videos and finished output are stored and processed locally on your PC and are never uploaded anywhere. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
Do I need an NVIDIA graphics card?
No. Any GPU that supports Microsoft's DirectML and D3D12 works — NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, including many integrated GPUs. The app also runs fine on low-end PCs, just more slowly.
How much can it upscale a video by?
Up to roughly 4x a video's native resolution in a single fast pass, and higher targets like 4K or 8K are reached automatically using tiled processing.
Can it increase frame rate too?
Yes — it can raise a video from a typical 24–30fps up to 45 or 60fps by generating new, AI-interpolated in-between frames, at the same time as upscaling resolution. That combination is ideal for old, low-quality phone videos.
Can I process more than one video at a time?
Yes — queue several videos and AI Video Upscaler+ processes them one after another in the background, with a Windows notification when each one finishes.
