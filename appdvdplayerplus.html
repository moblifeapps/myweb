DVD Player+ Features

More than a usual DVD Player!

DVD Player+ — Your complete, watermark-free DVD playback solution for Windows.
Enjoy your movie collection with full DVD menu navigation, subtitle support, audio-track selection, and smooth hardware-accelerated playback. DVD Player+ is lightweight, fast— without watermarks, time limitations or registrations.
Play all your VIDEO_TS, DVDs, VCDs, Audio CDs.
Play Blu-Ray Movies (more than 20k titles supported).

🎬 Key Features

• Full DVD Menu Support — Navigate title, chapter, and special-feature menus just like on a standalone DVD player.
• Subtitle & Audio Track Switching — Select your preferred language or subtitle instantly.
• External Subtitle support - Bring your own subtitle file. Sync the subtitle and the movie easily if needed.
• Chapter Navigation & Resume Playback — Jump to any scene or continue where you left off.
• Hardware Acceleration — Optimized decoding for smooth playback and lower CPU use.
• Region & Format Support — Compatible with most DVD regions and VIDEO_TS structures.
• Phone Remote Control - use your phone as a remote control when watching (iPhones, Android, iPad). No need for same WiFi network (PC & phone).
• Multi-Language Interface — Localized menus and subtitles for an international audience.
• Clean & Simple Interface — No watermark, no clutter, just your movie.


💡 Why Choose DVD Player+

Many free players watermark video or require registration. DVD Player+ focuses entirely on performance and clarity. It automatically detects your DVD drive or folder structure and starts playback instantly.

Whether you’re watching standard discs, backups, or disc copies (VIDEO_TS or VCDs), DVD Player+ delivers a dependable, high-quality experience.

⚙️ System Requirements

• Windows 10 or Windows 11 (x64/x86)
• DVD drive or CD Drive, or Blu-Ray Drive
• DirectX 11+ GPU recommended for hardware acceleration
