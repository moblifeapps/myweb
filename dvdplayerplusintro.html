The App

DVD Player+ Features



DVD Player+ is a High-quality, 100% Free, with no-watermarks over movie like other DVD players, specially designed for playback of your Movies or Media Files that are stored in your CD's, DVD's, Blu-Ray or VCD's you may have. You may use your phone as a Remote Control Playback!
Features
- Read and play movies and media files from CD-Rom, DVD-Rom, Audio-CD, Blu-Ray, VCD's etc.
- Play almost every Movie and Media Video or Audio Format that exists (over 200 media formats are supported!)
- Automatically load all Media (Video/Audio) Disc contents during swap of DVD’s
- 14 High-Quality, custom-made Visual Effects for the Video.
- A real-time 2D to 3D conversion during playback, so the 2D Video is viewed as a 3D one (with anaglyph glasses)
- Adjust the brightness, the saturation and the contrast of the video (for dark videos)
- Rotate the video with 6 options (vertically, flip horizontally, etc)
- Capture snapshots from the video and auto-save them to the Pictures folder during playback
- Replay, Play, Pause, Move back or forward, Skip positions
- Full Screen View or Normal View
- Adjust the volume
- Switch modes without stop
- (Movie Player) External Subtitles support
- (Movie Player) Switch Chapters
- (Movie Player) Movie Full Information
- Supports 6 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Greek!
