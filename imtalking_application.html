The Technology

ImTalking is built on two real, named AI models — the same class of technology powering cloud-based AI avatar tools.

SadTalker

An AI model trained to map speech onto realistic facial movement — lip sync, expression, and natural head motion generated frame by frame from your audio.

An optional AI enhancement pass that sharpens and cleans up the final frame, restoring detail that the animation process can soften.

FAQ

Common questions

Is ImTalking really free?
Yes. Every feature is available with no subscription, no per-video charges, and no watermark on your videos.
Does ImTalking send my photos or videos anywhere?
No. Photos, generated audio, and finished videos are stored and processed locally on your PC and are never uploaded anywhere. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
Is my typed text ever sent anywhere?
Only when you use Text-to-Speech mode: the text you type is sent to Microsoft's Edge TTS service to generate the audio. Audio File mode avoids this entirely, since you supply your own recording.
How many voices are available?
320+ neural voices across dozens of languages, each previewable before you generate a video.
What is GFPGAN / Enhance?
An optional face-restoration pass that sharpens the final video, at the cost of extra render time.
Can I generate more than one video at a time?
Yes — queue several videos and ImTalking renders them one after another in the background, with a Windows notification when each one finishes.
