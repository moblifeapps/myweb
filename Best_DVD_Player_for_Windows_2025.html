The Best Free DVD Player for Windows 11: A Complete 2025 Guide

Introduction

In the world of entertainment, despite the widespread adoption of streaming technologies, millions of users worldwide still own DVDs, Blu-rays, VCDs, and audio CDs, and they want to play their media on Windows 11 computers. However, Windows 11 removed the native DVD playback capability, forcing users to look for alternatives. The truth is that most users end up disappointed—many free options are outdated, limited, slow, or filled with ads.

This guide covers the best and most reliable way to play DVDs and Blu-rays on Windows 11 in 2025, including why DVD Player+ has become a popular choice for users who want a free, modern, and fully capable solution for playing their optical discs.

Why Windows 11 No Longer Plays DVDs

Historically, Microsoft removed DVD playback support after Windows 7, mainly due to:

• licensing costs
• declining disc usage
• shift toward streaming services
• low demand from OEM laptop manufacturers

This left Windows users searching for a full replacement that supports menus, subtitles, Blu-ray, and more.

Why You Need a Dedicated Player

In practice, dragging a VIDEO_TS folder into a random media player does NOT provide:

• menus
• chapters
• extra features
• audio track switching
• subtitle selection

A proper DVD player should offer all of the above.

Introducing DVD Player+

Among the available options, DVD Player+ stands out as a free, modern, and reliable DVD and Blu-ray player for Windows 11.
