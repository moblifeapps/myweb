How to download and use them

  1. Open the gallery and pick a category, or search for what you like.
  2. Click Download on the video you like. It is saved to your Downloads folder.
  3. Open the Downloads folder, right-click the file and choose Add to Desktop Live Wallpaper+. It starts playing behind your icons.

Videos in categories

Browsing by category is a very good idea, and that's why the gallery is organized that way. We believe that downloading and using videos from the same category together gives your desktop Timeline a pretty cool look and feel. If you have time and want to try it, use 5 or 6 videos from the Nature category and order them to play one after another. Then add your other favorite photos in order on the same Timeline (videos together, photos together) and check the final result!

Preview before you download

Rest the mouse on a video in the gallery to see it move, or click it to watch it larger with its size, length and author. Search by subject too – ocean, rain, city at night…

Choose the right size

Each video shows its quality (HD, Full HD, 2K, 4K). On an older PC prefer Full HD – see the tips on the Usage & Tips page.

License notice: what kind of videos should I use?

Videos that are publicly available on a web page almost always come with a license for the end user, and such licenses exist for almost all web resources – videos, photos, pictures and wallpapers. From our part, we strongly advise the users of Desktop Live Wallpaper+ to respect the licenses!
