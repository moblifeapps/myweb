Three layers, combined: line, background, buttons

Most taskbar tools give you one switch: transparent or not. Transparent Taskbar+ Lite builds your taskbar from three layers and draws them together, from the bottom up:

1 · Background

Transparent, a one-colour gradient, a two-colour 50-50 gradient, a pattern, or your own picture.

2 · Buttons

Plain icons, glass buttons, colour buttons, a glass dock or a colour dock behind your app icons.

3 · Border line

No line, or a thin (1 px) or thick (2 px) line in Windows' own text colour or any colour you pick.

Every click previews the full combination on your real taskbar for 5 seconds. Press Apply to keep it - nothing changes until you do.

Your own picture on the taskbar

Turn any PNG or JPG into your taskbar background - a texture, a photo strip, your brand colours. The app keeps its own copy, so you can move or delete the original afterwards.

  • Fill, Stretch, Tile or Center - choose how the picture fits the taskbar.
  • Opacity - fade the picture into a solid backing colour (automatic from the picture, black, or custom).
  • Side taskbars - use a different, tall picture when the taskbar is on the left or right.
  • Size hint - the app tells you your taskbar's exact size in pixels, so you can prepare a sharp picture.

50+ patterns, ready to use

Four families of hand-tuned patterns: Dark subtle textures, Light textures, Thin Glow fine grids with soft blue beams, and Heavy Glow & Beams for a bold look. Click one to see it on your taskbar.

Icon magnifier and mouse effects

Icon magnifier (on by default) makes the app icon under the mouse grow out of the taskbar on a small glass bubble, like a dock - live, smooth, and your clicks still go straight to the icon.

Taskbar highlight lights the taskbar when the mouse arrives, Click highlight adds a small bubble burst on each click, and Mouse light shadow follows the pointer with a soft glow. Each is a simple on/off switch; these three are off until you turn them on. Read the Effects Guide →

How to start in one minute

  1. Install from the Microsoft Store

    Get the app for free and open it. It starts in the tray, next to the clock.

  2. Open the settings

    The settings open in your browser (or in the app window - your choice). Approve the one-time browser prompt that lets the page talk to the app on your PC.

  3. Pick a line, a background and buttons

    Click a card in each row. Your taskbar shows the combination for 5 seconds.

  4. Press Apply

    The style is saved and comes back every time Windows starts (if Start with Windows is on).

Frequently asked questions

Is Transparent Taskbar+ Lite free?

Yes. It is free on the Microsoft Store for Windows 10 and Windows 11, with no in-app purchases needed for any style or effect.

Is it a good alternative to TranslucentTB?

Yes, if you want more than transparency. TranslucentTB focuses on clear, blur and acrylic modes that change with window state. Transparent Taskbar+ Lite has tint, blur and acrylic too, and adds a styled border line, one- and two-colour gradients, more than 50 patterns, your own pictures, glass or coloured buttons and docks behind your app icons, and mouse effects.

Can I use my own picture as the taskbar background?

Yes. Choose any PNG or JPG, then pick Fill, Stretch, Tile or Center, set the opacity and a backing colour. You can even use a different picture when the taskbar is on the left or right side of the screen.

Does it work with the taskbar at the top, left or right?

Yes. Move the taskbar in Windows Settings (Personalization > Taskbar > Taskbar behaviors > Taskbar position) and the app follows it automatically.

Does it support multiple monitors?

Yes. The style you choose is drawn on the taskbar of every monitor.

Can I combine styles?

Yes. Pick one border line, one background and one button style - they are drawn together, for example a thin line, a picture background and a glass dock.

Do I have to install anything in my browser?

No. The settings open in the app's own window or in your normal browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Vivaldi). The browser only talks to the app on your own PC.

Can taskbar icons grow under the mouse like a Mac dock?

Yes. The Icon magnifier effect (on by default) makes the icon under the mouse grow out of the taskbar on a glass bubble. Only the icon you point at grows, so clicks always land on the right app. Turn it off in the Effects tab.

Does it slow Windows down?

No. Styles are drawn once and only redrawn when something changes, so the app uses very little CPU while you work.

How do I remove it?

Exit it from the tray icon, or uninstall it like any Microsoft Store app. Your normal Windows taskbar comes back.
