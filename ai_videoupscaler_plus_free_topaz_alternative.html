A free alternative to Topaz Video AI for Windows

Topaz Video AI is a powerful, paid tool for professionals. If you mainly want to upscale and smooth your own videos for free, AI Video Upscaler+ may be all you need. Here is an honest comparison.

Short answer

Is there a free alternative to Topaz Video AI?

Yes. AI Video Upscaler+ is free, with no watermark, for the most common upscaling jobs.

AI Video Upscaler+ covers the three things most people want from an AI video enhancer: upscaling (Real-ESRGAN, up to 8K), smoother motion (RIFE frame interpolation to 45 or 60 fps) and face restoration (GFPGAN). Which one?

How to choose

AI Video Upscaler+ is enough if you…

  • want old or personal videos in 4K, for free
  • want choppy 24–30 fps footage smooth at 60 fps
  • want sharper faces in family videos
  • have an ordinary PC or a laptop with integrated graphics

Topaz Video AI is better if you…

  • need many specialised models and fine control
  • work professionally on client or film footage
  • need macOS, or features beyond upscaling, fps and faces
  • are happy to pay for a professional tool
